Edit the text of any page

The bar in the bottom-right corner is the editor, running on this page. Turn on “Edit text”, click this paragraph and type. Enter keeps the change, Shift+Enter adds a line break, and Escape undoes the edit.

On another website

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:
Edit text
  1. Open any page and click the bookmark.
  2. Your changes are saved in this browser for that page, and they come back the next time you click the bookmark there. “Changes” lists every edit, old → new, ready to copy.

To have the editor on a site you build yourself, add <script src="text-editor.js"></script>.

Switching between variants

Wrap design options in data-variants, one child per option with data-variant. “Options” in the bar then steps through them, one at a time, and remembers your pick:

<div data-variants="Button">
  <div data-variant="Solid">…</div>
  <div data-variant="Outline">…</div>
</div>

Changing spacing

Turn on “Spacing” and click any element. The panel shows its margin, padding and gap in px, and the page draws the margin in orange and the padding in green. Drag a field left or right like in Figma, type a value, click − + or use ↑ ↓ (Shift for steps of 8); “↑ Parent” moves to the element around it. Every change shows up under “Changes”.
